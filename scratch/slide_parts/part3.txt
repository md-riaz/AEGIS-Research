<!-- SLIDE 8: What This Thesis Proposes -->
<div class="slide" id="s8">
<h2>What This Thesis Proposes</h2>
<div class="two-col">
<div>
<h3>Core Idea</h3>
<p style="font-size:1.1em;color:var(--text);font-weight:500;margin-bottom:16px">AI understands the question.<br>The <strong>system</strong> controls the execution.</p>
<ul>
<li>LLM extracts structured intent (JSON), never SQL</li>
<li>Semantic layer maps business terms to schema</li>
<li>Template compiler generates safe SQL</li>
<li>Visualization selected by policy rules</li>
<li>Output persisted as refreshable widget</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div>
<h3>AI Freedom Boundary</h3>
<div class="card" style="border-left:3px solid var(--green)">
<h3 style="color:var(--green);font-size:.95em">AI CAN &#9989;</h3>
<p style="font-size:.88em">Rephrase intent &bull; Infer missing context &bull; Choose best metric &bull; Decide chart type &bull; Suggest follow-ups</p>
</div>
<div class="card" style="margin-top:12px;border-left:3px solid var(--red)">
<h3 style="color:var(--red);font-size:.95em">AI CANNOT &#10060;</h3>
<p style="font-size:.88em">Invent joins &bull; Bypass permissions &bull; Hit raw tables &bull; Create unbounded queries &bull; Generate SQL directly</p>
</div>
<p style="margin-top:12px;font-weight:600;font-size:.95em;color:var(--accent)">Freedom of thinking, not acting.</p>
</div>
</div>
</div>

<h3>Formal Model</h3>
<p style="font-size:.9em"><code>g(q, L, r) &rarr; &langle;&pi;, sql, vis, w&rangle;</code><br><br>
<strong>&pi;</strong> = analysis plan &bull; <strong>sql</strong> = compiled query &bull; <strong>vis</strong> = chart spec &bull; <strong>w</strong> = persisted widget<br><br>
Safety: <code>sql &isin; Q_safe(L, r)</code> &mdash; all queries bounded by semantic layer L and role r.</p>
</div>
<div class="card">
<h3>Five Design Principles</h3>
<ul style="font-size:.88em">
<li>Interpret and execute separately</li>
<li>Make business semantics explicit</li>
<li>Bound the executable space</li>
<li>Guide visualization by policy</li>
<li>Persist outputs as artifacts</li>
</ul>
