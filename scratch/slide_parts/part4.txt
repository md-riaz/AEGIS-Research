<h3 style="margin-top:20px">Example Mapping</h3>
<p style="font-size:.9em">User: <em>"Show top 5 products by sales last month"</em></p>
<ul style="font-size:.88em">
<li>Metric: <code>total_sales</code> &rarr; SUM(oi.PriceExclTax * oi.Quantity)</li>
<li>Dimension: <code>product_name</code> &rarr; p.Name</li>
<li>Time: <code>last_month</code> &rarr; DATEADD(month, -1, ...)</li>
<li>Pattern: <code>ranking</code> &rarr; ORDER BY + TOP 5</li>
<li>Output: Bar chart widget</li>
</ul>
</div>

<li><code>mapper.py</code> &mdash; Semantic mapping + viz defaults</li>
